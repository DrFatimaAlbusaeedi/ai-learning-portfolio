## 📚 About the course

A 12-week programme combining lectures with a capstone project. It covers:

- 🧮 Mathematics for data science: linear algebra, probability and statistics
- 📊 Data preprocessing and visualization with NumPy and Pandas
- 🔍 Supervised and unsupervised machine learning
- 💬 Natural language processing and text mining
- ✍️ Prompt engineering and foundation models across AI platforms
- 🧠 Neural networks and deep learning with TensorFlow and Keras (CNN, RNN, GAN)
- 🤗 Practical use and fine-tuning of large language models with Hugging Face
- ⚖️ AI ethics, responsible AI and risk management
- 🚀 A team capstone project using open-source data

## 🔬 Research interests

- 🤝 **Human–AI interaction at work:** how people understand, trust and use AI-enabled tools in organizational settings.
- 📢 **Communicating AI-generated information:** how communication and visual presentation support understanding and decision-making.
- 🏛️ **Stakeholder needs and workflows:** how organizational context shapes the adoption of AI and media intelligence systems.
- 🧑‍💻 **Human-centered AI:** trust, transparency, usability and accessibility in intelligent software.

### ❓ Questions I'm exploring

*Illustrative research directions, not completed studies.*

1. How can intelligent interfaces communicate decisions and recommendations in ways users understand and trust?
2. How does interface design influence users' confidence, behavior and adoption of AI-enabled software?
3. How can user-centered research inform more accessible and intuitive intelligent systems?
